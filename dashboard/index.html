What is “real salary”? — Real salary adjusts your nominal (contractual) salary for inflation, expressing it in 2023 purchasing power using the Swiss Consumer Price Index (LIK/IPC) published by the Federal Statistical Office (BFS/OFS). A real increase means your salary bought more goods and services than in 2023; a real decrease means the opposite. Formula: Real salary = Nominal salary × CPI2023 / CPIcurrent
